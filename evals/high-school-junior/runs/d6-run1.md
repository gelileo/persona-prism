### performed-understanding
> This coincidence of angle is the source of a persistent practical hazard, since fasteners of similar nominal diameter drawn from the two systems can be started into one another by hand and will engage across several turns before binding.

Metric and Unified both use the sixty-degree angle, so the profile geometry is the same and they only differ in the dimensioning of the diameters and the pitches. That's why a metric one and an inch one of about the same nominal diameter will start into each other by hand and go several turns before they bind.
