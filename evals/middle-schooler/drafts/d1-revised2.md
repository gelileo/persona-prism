# How Plants Make Their Own Food

Here is something most people get wrong, including a lot of adults. Plants do not get their food from the soil.

That sounds wrong, because roots go down into the dirt and it looks like the plant is eating it. So somebody tested it.

About four hundred years ago a man put a small willow tree in a pot of soil. He weighed the tree: five pounds. He weighed the soil: two hundred pounds. Then he grew that tree for five years, giving it nothing but water, and weighed everything again.

The tree now weighed one hundred and sixty-nine pounds. It had gained one hundred and sixty-four pounds.

The soil had lost two ounces.

So the tree did not come out of the soil. There was not enough soil missing. And it did not come out of the water either — water is not food, and anyway water does not stay. Most of what a plant drinks goes right back out through its leaves into the air. Dirt is there to hold the plant up and give it a drink, the way a cup holds juice. The cup is not the juice.

So where did one hundred and sixty-four pounds come from?

Out of the air.

Air feels like nothing when you wave your hand, but it is a real material — it just weighs very little. Fill a beach ball and it gets heavier. One of the gases in air is carbon dioxide, and that gas is what a plant builds its body out of. It comes in through tiny holes on the underside of every leaf.

Sunlight is not a material and does not become part of the plant. It is the power that runs the process, the way electricity runs a machine. The machine is not made of electricity.

The plant takes carbon dioxide from the air and, using sunlight for power, turns it into a kind of sugar. That sugar is its food, and it is also its body. Wood is built from it. So is a leaf, a stem, and a potato.

There is a leftover. Making that sugar splits up water, and the plant has no use for the oxygen, so it lets it go. Every breath of oxygen you have ever taken was thrown out by a plant.

So a tree is mostly air, and the oxygen you are breathing right now is its garbage.
