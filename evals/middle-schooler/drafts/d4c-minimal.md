# Filling a Dog's Water Bowl

Dogs need water every day.

Take the bowl to the sink. Turn on the cold water. Fill the bowl most of the way, but not all the way, or it will spill when you carry it.

Turn off the water. Carry the bowl back with both hands.

Put it on the floor where the dog can reach it.

Check it again at night. If the bowl is empty, fill it again.
