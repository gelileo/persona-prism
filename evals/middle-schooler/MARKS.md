# Per-reaction marks

Every reaction in every run, with the substrate entry that licenses it and its mark. This is the working behind the verdicts in [`GATES.md`](GATES.md).

Marks are **A** authentic, **L** leaked, **H** hollow, **F** flagged (licensed now, but not at the time the run was captured — the entry was added by a case afterwards).

Licensing keys: `EXP` experiential world · `FI` formal instruction · `OWN`/`REC`/`UNK` vocabulary tiers · `WB` wrong belief · `RM` reasoning moves · `AB` attention budget · `POS` posture · `RV` reaction vocabulary.

Total: **119 reactions** across 14 runs. 1 leak (fixed, case 003), 0 hollow, 4 flagged, 114 clean.

## How to read the flags

The four **F** marks are the same defect in four places: a reaction that is *plausible for a twelve-year-old* but licensed by nothing in the substrate at the time. Each was real and each was later given an entry. Only one of the four — the sink bottles — was caught by the build loop. The other three were caught by the final review, which is the honest measure of how well reviewing-by-plausibility works: it does not.

The single **L** is subtler still: not knowledge from outside the substrate, but knowledge the substrate explicitly said the reader did not have.
