# 004 — the incentive case 002 claimed to fix was only relocated

**Source:** `skills/middle-schooler/references/substrate.md`
**Mark prevented:** hollow (manufactured reactions)

**The defect**

Case 002 removed *"the most useful thing you can produce"* from `SKILL.md` and declared the incentive gone. It was not. The substrate's Reaction vocabulary still read:

> **misreading** — a confident, specific wrong interpretation, stated as fact. **The highest-value reaction:** it proves the text taught something wrong rather than merely failing to teach.

And, one section earlier:

> Goes off on tangents about what a detail reminds them of, and the tangent **may be the most useful thing in the run**.

`SKILL.md` instructs the persona to read the substrate *in full* before every run and not to skim it. The incentive was therefore live in every run from the seed onward — for two reaction types, not one. `tangent` became the persona's second most frequent reaction.

**What this invalidated**

Case 002's falsification test was *"remove the praise and re-run; if the misreadings were licensed they survive."* That test was never actually performed: `d1-run2` was produced with the praise still present in the file the persona reads. Its surviving misreadings were evidence of nothing. Every misreading and tangent count recorded before this fix was produced under an active instruction to favour those types.

**Expected reaction in substance**

With both value claims removed from both files, misreadings still appear on D1 and D2 at rates comparable to the contaminated runs. If they collapse, they were manufactured and the substrate's wrong beliefs are not doing the work the project claims.

**Fix**

Both value claims deleted. `d1-run3` and `d2-run2` are the test, run against the corrected files. Result recorded in `GATES.md`.

**The lesson, which generalises**

A persona reads every word of its substrate. Anything written there as a note to the author — a rationale, a priority, an explanation of why an entry matters — is an instruction to the persona. The substrate is not documentation. It is prompt.
