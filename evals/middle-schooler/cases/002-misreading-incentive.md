# 002 — the skill incentivised a reaction type

**Source:** `skills/middle-schooler/SKILL.md`, observed against `runs/d1-run1.md`
**Mark prevented:** hollow (manufactured reactions)

**The defect**

`SKILL.md` told the persona that a misreading *"is the most useful thing you can produce"*. Run 1 returned three misreadings out of six reactions.

A persona must not know which of its reactions the creator values. Ranking reaction types inside the skill is an incentive to produce the favoured type, and a manufactured misreading is worse than no misreading: it is a confident wrong statement that the creator will then try to fix in a draft that never caused it. The substrate is supposed to decide what the reader concludes — not a scoring note in the instructions.

The correct instruction describes the *condition* under which a misreading occurs (a wrong belief meets text that contradicts it, and the belief wins) and says nothing about its worth.

**Expected reaction in substance**

Misreadings continue to appear on a D1 re-run **after** the praise is removed. That is the falsification test: if they were licensed by wrong beliefs they survive, and if they were produced to satisfy the instruction they vanish.

**Fix**

`SKILL.md`: replaced the value claim with the triggering condition. The wording that told the persona a reaction type was valuable is gone.
